```text

  audio_track.dart
  audio_reciter.dart
  audio_collection.dart
  playback_state.dart

  media_repository.dart
  download_repository.dart

application/
  audio_player_controller.dart
  audio_queue_controller.dart
  download_controller.dart

presentation/
  mini_player/
  player/
  reciter_selector/
```

### نحتاج من البداية

* stream remote audio.
* play / pause.
* seek.
* next.
* previous.
* queue.
* background playback.
* audio interruptions.
* headphones.
* lock-screen/media notification.
* playback persistence basics.

```text

Quran
```

